Honk Out! Support

Stuck in a jam? We're here to help.

Honk Out! is a colourful car puzzle game for all ages. Clear packed parking lots, pick up the people waiting at the stops, and drive through 300 clever levels in fun places like the beach, a snowy village and the city at night.

How do I play?

Look at the people waiting at the stop and tap a car of the same colour: it drives out of the lot and picks them up. A car of the wrong colour waits in the Waiting Bay until its people come, so don't fill the bay up. If a car is blocked it bumps, and every bump uses one Honk. Plan your moves, use UNDO to take back a move, and use boosters like HINT and TOW TRUCK when you need a hand.

I reinstalled the game and my progress is gone. Why?

Your progress is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling the game removes it. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may restore your progress when you reinstall the game. Otherwise, the game starts again from Level 1.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear button to open Settings, then tap the SOUND, MUSIC or VIBRATE button to switch it off or on.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings in the game and tap PRIVACY OPTIONS. If no choices are needed where you live, the game will tell you. See our Privacy Policy for more.

A video won't play, or I see "NO VIDEO NOW". What can I do?

Ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available for a moment. Check that you are online, wait a little and try again. The game still works without ads: you can always keep playing, and rewarded videos are only ever your choice.
