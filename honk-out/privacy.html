Honk Out! Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters, even in a traffic jam.

Effective date: 27 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no login, no chat. We never ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your phone.
  • We store only whether you are under 13 or 13 and older, never your birth year.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Players under 13 only see non-personalized, child-friendly ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always your choice.

1. Who we are

Honk Out! is a car puzzle game made by NPM Tech Solutions. You can contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

2. What we don't collect

Honk Out! has no accounts, no login and no chat. The game does not ask players for their name, email address, photos, contacts or location, and we don't collect any of these.

3. What the game saves on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: levels, stars, coins, settings (sound, music and vibration), boosters, Garage items, gifts and which tips you've already seen. It also keeps simple counters and times on the phone (for example when the last ad was shown), so it doesn't show ads too often. This information stays on your phone and is not sent to us.

Uninstalling the game deletes this saved progress. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up the game's progress to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup is controlled by your phone and Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

4. The age question

The first time you open the game, a neutral age screen asks for your year of birth. The game only stores the result, under 13 or 13 and older, on your phone. The birth year itself is not stored and is never sent anywhere. The result is used only to choose the right kind of ads (see section 6).

5. Ads and Google AdMob

Honk Out! is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner, full-screen ads between levels, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always optional: they only play when you tap a button that offers a reward, and you can always say no.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app;
  • how you interact with ads;
  • crash and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us. You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Players under 13

When a player is under 13:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed and as under the age of consent;
  • only non-personalized ads are shown;
  • the maximum ad content rating is G (suitable for general audiences);
  • no app-open ads are shown.

Honk Out! follows the Google Play Families Policy and uses only ad SDKs that are self-certified for Google Play Families.

7. Players 13 and older: your privacy choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), Google's consent message lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choices any time in the game under Settings > Privacy Options.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalization in your phone's settings (on Android, usually under Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

8. Your data rights

You may have the right to access your data, ask us to delete it, or object to how it is used. We don't hold any personal data on our own servers: everything the game saves is on your phone, and uninstalling the game removes it (unless Android backup restores it, see section 3). For data used by Google for ads, you can use Google's own controls, described in Google's Privacy Policy. For any question about your data, email us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

9. Children's privacy

Honk Out! is made to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. NPM Tech Solutions does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, no chat and no way to share personal information, and ads for players under 13 are handled as described in section 6. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns, please contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com and we will help.

10. Security

Your game data is stored on your phone and protected by your phone's own security. Ad data is handled by Google under its own policies and security measures.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Please check this page from time to time.
