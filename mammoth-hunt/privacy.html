Mammoth Hunt Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Mammoth Hunt game uses, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Mammoth Hunt is made by NPM Tech Solutions. You can contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your game progress, coins, upgrades and settings (such as sound, music and vibration) on your device, so you can continue where you left off. This information stays on your device.

The game has no accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address or any other contact details.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • information about your device and the app
  • your IP address and approximate location
  • how you interact with ads

This information is collected by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent

If you live in a region where consent is required (for example, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy Options button in the game's Settings.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (on Android, usually under Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads; on iPhone, under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Children

Mammoth Hunt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. For any other questions or requests about your data, email us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

Security

Your game data is kept on your device, and the ads are provided by Google using its own security measures. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we only use well-known, trusted services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
