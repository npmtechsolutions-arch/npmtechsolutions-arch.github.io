Mammoth Hunt Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters, even on the hunt.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • Your game progress is saved on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device information to show and measure ads.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices anytime in the game's Settings.

1. Who we are

Mammoth Hunt is made by NPM Tech Solutions. You can contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

2. What the game saves on your device

The game saves your progress, coins, upgrades, settings (such as sound, music, vibration and less flashing) and which tips you've already seen, on your phone, so you can carry on where you left off. It also keeps simple counts of how many ads it has shown, to help us improve the game. All of this stays on your phone.

We don't collect your name, email address or contacts, and there is no account or sign-in.

3. Ads and Google AdMob

Mammoth Hunt is free and shows banner, full-screen (interstitial) and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always your choice: they only play when you tap a button that offers a reward.

To show, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID;
  • information about your device and the app;
  • your IP address and approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads.

This information is collected by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your privacy choices

In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime with the Privacy Options button in the game's Settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalisation in your phone's settings (on Android, usually under Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads; on iPhone, under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

5. Backup of your progress

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up your game progress to your Google account, so it can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This is controlled by your phone and Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

6. Children

Mammoth Hunt is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information, please contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com and we will help.

7. Keeping and deleting data

We don't store any of your data on our own servers. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress saved on your phone (unless Android backup restores it, see section 5). For anything else, email us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

8. Security

Your game data is stored on your phone and protected by your phone's own security. Ad data is handled by Google under its own policies and security measures.

9. Changes to this policy

When anything changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.
