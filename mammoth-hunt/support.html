Mammoth Hunt Support

Need help on the hunt? We're here.

How do I play?

Drag left and right to steer your tribe. Go through the blue gates to grow your tribe, and dodge rocks, spikes and the red zones when a mammoth attacks. Your hunters throw spears by themselves. You can see this any time in Settings > How to Play.

How do I replay old levels?

On the start screen, tap the map button next to the gear (it says LEVELS under it). Tap any level you've already beaten, and it starts straight away. Replaying never changes your progress.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Mammoth Hunt free for everyone. A short ad plays between levels, and a small banner shows at the bottom of the screen. Rewarded ads are always your choice: they only play when you tap a "Watch ad" button for a reward.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings in the game (the gear button) and tap Privacy Options. If no choices are needed where you live, the game will tell you. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, your progress may be restored when you reinstall. Otherwise, the game starts again from Level 1.

How do I reset my progress?

There is no reset button in the game yet. To start again from Level 1, open your phone's Settings > Apps > Mammoth Hunt > Storage and tap Clear storage (or uninstall and reinstall the game with Android backup turned off). To see the tips again without losing progress, use Settings > Reset Tips in the game.
