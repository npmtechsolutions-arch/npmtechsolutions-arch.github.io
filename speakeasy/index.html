What SpeakEasy does

  • Voice and conversation: speak and hear the translation, or talk face to face.
  • Camera: point at signs and menus, or translate a photo.
  • Documents: translate PDFs and letters, and ask questions about them.
  • Offline and private: translation happens on your phone. Your text is not sent to us.

Common questions

I bought Remove ads but still see ads

Open Settings → Privacy → Restore purchases (also on the Remove ads screen). Use the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with. If ads still show, email us and we'll help. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store.

A language says "Needs internet"

Translation works offline once the language pack is downloaded: go to Settings → Language downloads and download the language. "Needs internet" on voice input means your phone's speech service can only recognise that language online. On Android 13 or later, if an on-device model is offered for it, you can download it on the same screen.

How do I delete my data?

Everything SpeakEasy keeps is on your phone. Use Settings → Clear all data to delete your history, saved phrases and settings, or delete single items in History. Uninstalling the app also removes it. For ads, see Settings → Ad privacy choices and the privacy policy.
