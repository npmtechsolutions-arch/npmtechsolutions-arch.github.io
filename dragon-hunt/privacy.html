Dragon Hunt Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Dragon Hunt game uses, why, and the choices you have. Dragon Hunt is made for children and families, so we have kept the game's data use as small as we can, and this policy short and plain.

Who we are

Dragon Hunt (app id com.npmtechsolutions.dragonhunt) is made by NPM Tech Solutions. You can contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

The game collects no personal information

Dragon Hunt has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in. We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information, such as a name, email address, phone number, photos or location.

The game saves your game progress, coins, upgrades and settings (such as sound, music and vibration) on your device only, so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

Ads

Dragon Hunt is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Because children play the game, every ad request is set up to protect them:

  • every request is marked as directed to children (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (under European data protection law);
  • only non-personalised ads are shown: ads are never chosen based on what you do in other apps or websites;
  • only ads rated G (suitable for general audiences) are allowed;
  • the game does not use your device's advertising ID, and it never asks to track you across other apps.

To show ads, count them, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may still process some limited information, such as:

  • basic information about your device and the app (for example the device model, operating system and language);
  • your IP address, used to estimate a general region (such as the country);
  • whether an ad was shown or tapped.

This information is processed by Google, not by us, under Google's own rules for apps directed to children. You can read more here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

How ads appear in the game

Reward videos (for example extra coins or extra knights) only play when the player taps a clearly marked "WATCH AD" button. Watching ads is never needed to win. A full-screen ad may appear between levels, after a level is won (never after a lost level). A small banner ad is shown at the bottom of the screen, below the game picture, so it never covers the game.

For parents

Dragon Hunt is designed for children and families. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about this policy or about your child's use of the game, or if you believe any information about your child was collected by mistake, please email us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com. We will reply and help, including having any such information removed.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. For any other questions or requests about your data, email us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

Security

Your game data is kept on your device, and the ads are provided by Google using its own security measures. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we only use well-known, trusted services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
